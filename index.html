    CAN bus security testbed

    A virtual 3-ECU vehicle network (engine, brake, infotainment) built to run a full Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment against real CAN bus and UDS attack classes. It bridges hands-on CAN and UDS validation experience with formal ISO/SAE 21434 risk assessment and defence-layer design.

    Four attack classes tested on a simulated 3-ECU network.

    • Conducted a full TARA per ISO/SAE 21434, applying STRIDE and attack-tree analysis to score cross-ECU attack paths
